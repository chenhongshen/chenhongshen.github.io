Profile

My name is Hongshen Chen (陈宏申), and I am NLP lead for the JD.com recommendation platform, and a research scientist with Data Science Lab, JD.com. I also serve as a Youth Committee member of CCF TCCI (Technical Committee of Chinese Information, China Computer Federation). I received my Ph.D. from the NLP Group, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Qun Liu. Prior to that, I received my B.E. from Sichuan University in 2012 and worked as a short-term research assistant at Singapore University of Technology and Design with Prof. Yue Zhang, 2016. Currently, I am focusing on natural language processing, dialogue systems, machine translation, sequence labelling, etc. I won the best paper award on CCL 2016, and received the second prize of Wu Wenjun's artificial intelligence science and technology progress, Chinese society of artificial intelligence, 2022.

I maintain a list of my publications under the Publications tab, and a list of projects under the Projects tab. You may also check out my Google Scholar profile.
